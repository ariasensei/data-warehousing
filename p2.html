Lesson Overview

A Data Warehouse is a large storage system designed to collect information from many different sources into one central location. Instead of looking at multiple databases separately, organizations analyze everything from one place, making reporting faster and easier.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • ✔ Understand what a Data Warehouse is.
  • ✔ Explain why businesses use Data Warehouses.
  • ✔ Describe the ETL Process.
  • ✔ Identify the architecture layers.
  • ✔ Compare Operational Databases vs Data Warehouses.

💡 Why is it Important?

Imagine every department in a company stores information in different computers. A Data Warehouse gathers all of those records into one place, making it easier to generate reports and discover business trends.

🏢 Real World Examples

  • 🏦 Banks
  • 🛒 Online Shopping
  • 🏥 Hospitals

Easy Analogy

🍳 Think of Cooking

Source Systems are the ingredients.

The ETL Process is washing, cutting and cooking the ingredients.

The Data Warehouse is the refrigerator where everything is stored neatly.

Reports and Dashboards are the meals served to customers.

The Five Layers

1️⃣ Data Sources

Original data from websites, apps, files, or databases.

2️⃣ Staging Area

Temporary storage where data is checked and cleaned.

3️⃣ ETL Layer

Extract, Transform, and Load the information.

4️⃣ Data Warehouse

The central storage where cleaned data is kept.

5️⃣ Analytics Layer

Business Intelligence, Dashboards, Reports, Machine Learning, and Decision Making.

Class Activities

Try answering before revealing the solution.

Activity 1

A supermarket has information stored in Excel, Cash Registers, Online Orders, and Mobile Apps. Where should all of this information go before managers generate reports?

Answer: All data should go through the ETL Process and then be stored inside the Data Warehouse.

Activity 2

Identify whether each belongs to the Operational Database or Data Warehouse.

  • Monthly Sales Report
  • ATM Withdrawal
  • Business Dashboard
  • Cash Register Transaction

Monthly Sales Report → Data Warehouse

ATM Withdrawal → Operational Database

Dashboard → Data Warehouse

Cash Register → Operational Database

Activity 3

Arrange these in the correct order.

  • Reports
  • ETL
  • Data Sources
  • Warehouse

Data Sources ↓ ETL ↓ Data Warehouse ↓ Reports & Dashboards
